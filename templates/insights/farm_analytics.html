Which crops and herds make money?

How to read this: for each crop and herd, You spent is the total of the costs you recorded under Farm records (seed, fertiliser, feed, labour…). You earned is what buyers paid for stock you listed from that crop or herd. Profit = earned − spent.

If you still have stock to sell, the profit so far can look low or negative. The last line shows what your profit will be if the rest sells at today's market price.
