How it works

  1. A crop appears here on its expected harvest date.
  2. Click Start harvesting. The crop changes from Growing to Harvesting. Not allowed while a spray's pre-harvest interval is running.
  3. Record each harvest: date, quantity and grade.
  4. Click List for sale next to the harvest.
  5. When all of it is picked, click Finish harvesting. The crop becomes Completed.
